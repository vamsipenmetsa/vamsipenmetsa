About

Small Village → Warsaw

Born in Mogallu, a small remote village in Andhra Pradesh where the closest computer was a bus ride away, I build today in Warsaw. The path was anything but straight — Manual Tester, Automation, DevOps, SRE, Platform Engineer, and now AI Systems Architect building ML/LLM platforms for the world's largest financial institutions. Every pivot was a leap of faith. I share what I've learned because someone once did the same for me.
